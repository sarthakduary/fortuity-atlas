# Sources and editorial scope

Checked: 13 November 2026.

This collection contains verified arXiv hep-th records about fortuity and selected supporting literature. Discovery used searches for fortuity, fortuitous cohomology, monotone states, and black-hole cohomologies, followed by reference chaining, including the bibliography of arXiv:2611.04722. Current abstract pages were used for titles, authors, subject categories, summaries and original submission dates; HTML versions were used where needed.

It is a curated snapshot, not a guaranteed exhaustive search of all arXiv full text. The arXiv search endpoint could not be queried exhaustively in this run. Candidate 2609.35688 was not included because its primary-source page could not be retrieved. Generic incidental uses of the word fortuitous are excluded. Background entries are marked separately in the site. There is no automatic updating schedule.

## Classification

Core: papers developing, applying or testing fortuity, monotone/fortuitous cohomology or their direct mechanisms. Background: selected antecedents and directly related methods or microstate studies. Classifications and summaries are editorial. A paper’s presence is not a claim that its candidate states survive all quantum corrections.

## Version-sensitive correction

2505.14888 is listed under its revised title, Fortuity and Supergravity. Its current account identifies singleton states as monotone; the summary does not repeat the earlier classification.
