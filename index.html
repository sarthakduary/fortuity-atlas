02 / ABOUT THE ATLAS

A small distinction.
A rich landscape.

Fortuity describes BPS cohomology that exists at special finite ranks, rather than persisting in the same way as rank grows. The distinction between fortuitous and monotonous states offers a lens on the microscopic structure of supersymmetric black holes.

This collection brings together papers on fortuity and closely connected foundational work in arXiv’s hep-th category. Search covers titles, authors, arXiv identifiers, topics, and the short descriptions provided here.

Scope & inclusion criteria +

Entries are selected from searches for “fortuity,” “fortuitous,” and related BPS-cohomology terminology in titles and abstracts, together with relevant papers identified through references. Core papers directly investigate these questions; background papers supply important context. This is a reviewed bibliography, not a guarantee that every relevant paper has been captured. The review date is shown above.

All entries link to their arXiv source. Category, authorship, and dates are verified against source metadata. No unpublished or unverified manuscripts are included.
